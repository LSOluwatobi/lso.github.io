ABOUT

Analytics built around the question behind the data.

I focus on making reports useful to the person who has to read them, not just attractive on screen.

My work covers data preparation, KPI development, dashboard design, comparative analysis and visual storytelling. I enjoy taking a dataset from an untidy starting point and shaping it into something that makes patterns, differences and priorities easier to see.

The projects in this portfolio use Excel and Power BI across finance, healthcare, retail, aviation, hospitality, marketing, insurance, supply chain and sustainability.
